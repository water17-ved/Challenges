Memory & local backups

Export a JSON backup of your profile, joined challenge references, challenge definitions you can access, and your progress. Import a backup to restore profile settings and recover owned challenges as new copies where possible.

Backups are files you control. Keep them somewhere safe; imported owned challenges are recreated with new codes to avoid overwriting live challenges.
